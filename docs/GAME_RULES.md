# Broccoli Battle — The Rules

The official rules, in plain English, so they never become implementation
folklore.

## Portions

Every log is one food at one of three sizes:

| Size | Units | Meaning |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| SMALL | 0.5 | a little one |
| FIST | 1.0 | a normal fist-sized portion |
| MONSTER | 1.5 | an absurd amount |

Units are locked in when you log — changing the rules later never changes
history.

## The daily five

Your day's units add up; **5** is the goal. 4.5 is not five. 7 is showing
off (and still counts as one five-a-day). Days follow the household clock
(Europe/London by default) — a 23:59 carrot counts for today.

## The family target

Everyone's portions add up in the Battle Garden. **140 a week** (4 people ×
5 a day × 7 days) destroys the target. It is a shared achievement, never a
punishment.

## The weekly battle

- A game week runs **Monday 00:00 → Sunday 23:59:59.999** household time.
- Each week has exactly one battle, drawn **at random** from the active
  challenges. Nobody chooses it — not parents, not management. Repeats are
  allowed; that's the fun of a random draw.
- The six challenges: 🥦 **Veg King** (most veg portions), 🍓 **Fruit
  Champion** (most fruit portions), 🌈 **Variety Victory** (most different
  foods — eating the same food twice counts once), ⭐ **Five-a-Day Champion**
  (most days reaching five), 💥 **Portion Powerhouse** (most total
  portions), 🔥 **Streak Champion** (longest run of consecutive five-a-day
  days within the week).

## Winning, ties and empty weeks

- When the week ends the battle closes automatically and the results are
  frozen forever.
- Highest score wins the crown. A genuine tie means **JOINT CHAMPIONS** —
  no hidden tie-breakers, and every joint champion gets a spin.
- If everyone scores zero there is no champion and no spin. The next week's
  battle starts regardless.
- Once a battle closes, its entries are locked. Only management repair
  tools can touch history, and they should feel guilty doing it.

## The prize wheel

- Winning the weekly battle earns **exactly one** spin. It waits for you —
  there is no rush.
- The wheel holds the active prizes; heavier weights are bigger slices.
- The server decides the prize before the wheel finishes spinning.
  Refreshing the page does not, has never, and will never grant a second
  spin.

## Prize tickets

- A spin issues a **Prize Ticket** recording exactly what was won, for
  what, and when. Renaming a prize later never rewrites an old ticket.
- Tickets sit in your Prize Wallet until you press **CASH IT IN** — days,
  weeks, months, they do not expire.
- Cashing in stamps the ticket. It moves to your used pile and stays in the
  family record forever.

## The league

The league tracks each period (week, month, all time): portions, veg,
fruit, five-a-day days, streaks, variety, battle wins and perfect weeks.
Permanent titles (Veg Champion, Fruit Champion, Variety Champion, Streak
Leader, Five-a-Day Leader, Most Battle Wins) are bragging rights only — the
random weekly battle is the one that pays out prize spins.

## Perfect week

Five-a-day on all seven days, Monday to Sunday: a **PERFECT WEEK** 🏅. It's
an achievement and a league stat, not a prize spin.

## The collection

The first time anyone in the family eats a food, it is **discovered** and
its character card joins the collection, recording who its family champion
is. Undiscovered foods remain mysterious silhouettes.
